Prepshift is a bar-prep company. Our superjuice is made for bars and restaurants, produced with a facility partner and built for regional distribution. We develop small-batch products and training with a restaurant partner, keep a working manual of recipes and calculators, and help kitchens waste less.

Juice

Citrus superjuice for bar programs. It uses the peel as well as the juice, so a case of fruit goes further and holds longer than fresh-squeezed.
