Manual / Calculators

Cost & Yield Calculators

Scale any core recipe to any batch size. Get cost per ounce, per liter, and per cocktail pour in real time. Built for menu engineering and station prep lists — not for show.

How to use Pick a recipe, then enter a target yield in mL, fl oz or liters. Ingredient quantities and costs update as you type. Prices are reference defaults, so check them against your own invoices.

Specialized tools

Super Juice Calculator

Stretch a pound of citrus into three pounds of juice with a 2-week shelf life. Nickle Morris methodology, costed.

Batched Cocktail Scaler

Single-serving spec to any batch size, with proper dilution math, final ABV, container fit, and house ingredient cost.

Acid Substitution

Replace fresh citrus with water and food-grade acid. Arnold's methodology. Controversial — use sparingly, know what you're trading.

Menu Costing Engine

Enter your bottle costs once. See every drink on the menu costed, priced, and margin-analyzed instantly.

Draft & Bottled Cocktails

Force-carbonation, bottling protocol, draft line setup. Live calculator. The Morgenthaler methodology, fully documented.

Every tool a serious prep program actually needs, ranked by priority. Essentials, high-priority, useful, and advanced.
