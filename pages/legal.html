Legal

The plain-language version.

Alcohol

The manual covers recipes, methods and tools for preparing alcoholic drinks. It is written for adults of legal drinking age working in licensed settings. Nothing here encourages drinking by anyone under the legal age where they live.

If you or someone you know is struggling with alcohol, SAMHSA’s National Helpline in the United States is 1-800-662-4357. It is free, confidential and open 24 hours.

Food safety

The techniques in the manual involve raw fruit, fresh juice, raw egg white in some preparations, food-grade acids and vessels under pressure. Reading this site does not replace food handler training, a working knowledge of HACCP, or your local health department’s rules. If you operate commercially, your health department is the authority.

Calculators and reference values

The calculators and tables are provided as tools, without warranty. Costs, yields and hold times are reference values and vary by market, season and handling. Check them against your own invoices and your own product.

Credit and reuse

Where a recipe or method began with a named author, the credit appears on the relevant page: Nickle Morris and Kevin Kos for super juice, Jeffrey Morgenthaler for bottling and batching, Dave Arnold for dilution and acid adjustment, Sam Ross for honey-ginger syrup. If a credit is missing, that is an oversight. Tell us and we’ll fix it.

Advertising

There are no ads, affiliate links or paid placements on this site. Equipment and brand names appear only where they help explain a method.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The contact forms write an email in your own mail app; what you type is not sent to or stored by this site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Both receive ordinary request data, such as your IP address, when a page loads.
