§ 04 · The Origin Files

Stories from the route

How the methodology got built, what working with twenty bars at once taught me, and the slow accretion of small decisions that became the Prepshift method. These are the long-form companion pieces to the recipes — eventually the connective tissue of the book.

Status Stories is the lowest-priority section of this site by design. Long-form writing follows the recipes, the calculators, and the equipment list. The titles below are scaffolding for the book chapters; full pieces will publish here over the coming months.
§ Themes

What this section is for

Editorial position

The recipes on this site tell you what to do. The calculators tell you how much to do. The equipment list tells you what to do it with. Stories tell you why — and why is the part that survives when the specific tools and techniques get displaced by the next generation of them.

The bar industry has spent the last twenty years inheriting techniques from chemistry labs, kitchens, pharmacies, and Japanese craft tradition without quite knowing where any of it came from. Some of that is wonderful. Some of it is cargo-cult imitation that we keep doing because we saw someone else do it. Stories is the section where I will try to mark the difference, name the lineage where I know it, and admit the gaps where I don't.
