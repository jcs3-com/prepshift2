Cost & Yield Calculators

Scale any core recipe to any batch size. Get cost per ounce, per liter, and per cocktail pour in real time. Built for menu engineering and station prep lists — not for show.

How to use Pick a recipe below. Enter your target yield in mL, fl oz, or liters. The raw ingredient quantities and full cost breakdown will update instantly. All prices reflect placeholder data; replace with your wholesale costs in /data/ingredients.js until v1.0 ships.
