Fresh pressed juice and super juice for bars, restaurants and caterers.

What it is

Fresh pressed citrus juice, and super juice. Super juice uses the whole fruit. The peel’s oils are extracted and blended with the juice, so each case of citrus goes further and the result holds longer than fresh-squeezed.

It pours one-for-one in place of fresh juice.

The super juice method and maths are in the manual.

How it’s made

In batches, with our facility partner.

Who it’s for

Bars, restaurants, caterers, hotels and venues in the greater DC region.
